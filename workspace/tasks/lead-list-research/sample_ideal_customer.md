# Ideal customer: commercial grounds maintenance contracts

Seller: Harbor & Pine Landscaping, a 14-person landscaping company that wants year-round
commercial maintenance contracts to smooth out its seasonal residential work.

Who fits: property management companies that manage multi-unit residential buildings,
homeowner associations, or small office parks with shared outdoor grounds. Best fit is a
firm that manages ten or more properties, so one relationship can cover several sites.
Independent and regional firms fit better than national chains, which buy landscaping
through a central vendor program.

Region: Sacramento County, California.

Signals of a good fit, from public information: the firm's website lists the properties
or communities it manages; it mentions HOA management or multifamily management; it has a
local office in the region; it posts vendor or contractor information.
