# Preferences

Your standing rules. The agent reads this file at the start of every plan and applies each
entry whose "Applies when:" line matches the task. Edit these three examples to match how
you work, and add your own in the same shape. The first line of each entry, under its
heading, is always "Applies when:".

## Sign-off and voice
Applies when: writing any email, reply, or post that goes out under my name.
Sign as "Sam Rivera, Harbor & Pine Landscaping". Plain, warm, and short: no exclamation
points, no "just checking in", and one clear ask per message.

## Money and dates
Applies when: a draft or table mentions an amount owed, an invoice number, or a due date.
Copy amounts and invoice numbers from the source file with the same value, written with the
currency symbol, thousands separator, and two decimals (1240.00 becomes $1,240.00). Write dates as "October 12, 2026". When the source data looks
wrong, flag it for me and leave that customer's draft out.

## How I want results
Applies when: finishing any task run.
Lead the report with what needs my decision, most important first. Keep everything from a
run in its run folder, and keep summaries to one screen.
